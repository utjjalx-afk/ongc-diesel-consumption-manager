ONGC Mines Act Single-Entry Duty Logic Normal: 08:30 – 16:30 • ₹450 FD / Shift

Normal duty window is 08:30 to 16:30 (8.0 hours). If the job finishes within duty hours (e.g. 08:30 to 16:30), crew members receive Field Duty (FD) Allowance (₹450/shift), and OT = 0. If the shift starts before 08:30 or extends after 16:30, the system automatically computes both FD allowance and OT hours for each assigned member.
