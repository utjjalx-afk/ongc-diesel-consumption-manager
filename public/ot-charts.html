⚖️ Statutory Computation Basis

All Overtime allowances are calculated under the Mines Act, 1952 using the statutory rate formula: Hourly Rate = ⌊(Basic Pay + DA) / 104⌋ Field Duty Expenditure (FDE) is credited at a fixed rate of ₹450 per 8-hour shift for field operations.
